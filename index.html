About us

Built in Slovakia, for the places people actually go out

VoltsLine started with a problem every one of us has had: it is half past midnight, the night is still going, and your phone is dead. No photos, no taxi, no way to find the people you came with.

Shared power banks solved that in Madrid, Berlin and Brussels years ago. In Slovakia the service barely exists. We are building the network here — venue by venue, starting with the busiest nightlife and tourist spots in Bratislava.

We run our own hardware and our own software, so a partner venue gets one point of contact, and we can fix a station remotely instead of waiting on a supplier abroad.
